About Vimix

The Future of
Spatial Data

Vimix is pioneering the next dimension of sonar data visualization. Vimix streams live 3D models directly to AR glasses, replacing walls of screens with a single easily understandable immersive view.

Features

Why Vimix

Unlimited Control

Vimix lets you see through water wherever and whenever you look. No more staring at screens. Look, and you will see.

Superhuman Vision

Vimix gives you the superhuman ability to see through water. Think X-ray vision but for water. Instantly track targets, bottom structure, and more.

Custom AI Models

Our fine-tuned AI models enable autonomous, real-time target tracking. We custom train models for particular bodies of water.

Product

How It Works

01

Mount the Vimix Turret

Connect your forward-facing sonar to our smart turret, then mount it onto your trolling motor. It's a quick, two-bolt, plug-and-play setup built for active fishing.

02

Link Your AR Glasses

Wirelessly connect our augmented reality glasses in seconds. The onboard Vimix processor instantly converts your raw sonar feeds into real-time 3D spatial data.

03

Catch More Fish

See fish exactly where they actually are in the water, not on a flat screen. Seeing data clearly in full 3D makes locating and landing targets easier than ever.
