Process

How It Works

Three steps from raw data to spatial understanding.

01

Ingest & Fuse

Connect any data source — sonar, LiDAR, IoT sensors, telemetry feeds. Our fusion engine merges them into a unified spatial model in milliseconds.

02

Model & Render

Our 3D engine constructs a live mesh — complete with topology, highlighted objects, and data layers — optimized for real-time AR display.

03

Display & Act

Stream the live 3D model directly to AR glasses. See targets, structures, and anomalies overlaid on your real-world environment. Act with confidence.
